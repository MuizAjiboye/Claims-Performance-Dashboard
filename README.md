# 🚗 Claims Performance Dashboard
### High‑Performance Power BI Dashboard for Operational Insights

## 📄 Project Description
A cinematic, BCA‑inspired Power BI dashboard designed to centralize operational KPIs, data‑quality checks, and performance trends. Built to replace manual reporting, improve accuracy, and deliver fast, executive‑ready insights.

## 🎯 Purpose of the Project
- Provide a single source of truth for automotive operations  
- Automate reporting and reduce manual Excel work  
- Improve visibility into data quality and performance trends  
- Support leadership with real‑time, decision‑ready insights  

---

## 🧩 Business Problem
Operational teams lacked unified reporting, resulting in slow, inconsistent insights. Data quality issues (missing VINs, duplicates, invalid formats) created delays and reduced trust in analytics. Leadership needed a clear, automated view of performance and data health.

---

## 🎯 Dashboard Goal
Deliver a centralized, automated dashboard that surfaces KPIs, trends, and data‑quality indicators—enabling faster decisions, better forecasting, and improved operational transparency.

---

## 📊 Why These Visuals
- **KPI Cards** — Quick snapshot of critical metrics for leadership.  
- **Performance Bar Chart** — Highlights quarterly or monthly operational shifts.  
- **Trend Line Chart** — Reveals patterns, anomalies, and seasonality.  
- **Distribution Pie Chart** — Shows proportional breakdowns (regions, categories).  
- **Data Quality Indicators** — Flags missing values, invalid VINs, duplicates.  
- **Recent Activity Table** — Provides granular operational logs for investigation.  

Each visual was selected to maximize clarity, reduce cognitive load, and support fast decision‑making.

---

## ⭐ Business Impact
- 90% reduction in manual reporting effort  
- Improved data accuracy through automated quality checks  
- Faster detection of operational issues and anomalies  
- Clear, executive‑ready insights for strategic planning  
- A single, reliable source of truth for all teams  

---
